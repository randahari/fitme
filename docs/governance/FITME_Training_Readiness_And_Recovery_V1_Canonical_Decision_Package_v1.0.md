5. **Recovery/Rest Is Not An Activity** (extended — Planned Training Identity and Open-Ended Physical Activity, Chapter 09) — rest is a legitimate coaching action that must not be forced into the activity vocabulary merely to fit the current shape, and an absent activity identity must never be silently read as "safe." 6. **Two-Layer Safety** (extended — WALKING Safety and Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage, Chapter 10) — the model may receive Safety context before reasoning, but model awareness is never Safety authority; every actionable proposal remains subject to unconditional deterministic Safety review; Safety is extended only where a real structured/literal boundary requires it. Extended: WALKING's Canonical Safety Rule reuses RUNNING's own dimension profile; one new, generic `DEFERRED` Rule (the Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage Rule) applies whenever deterministic Safety compatibility cannot be established for a `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal — covering both an open-ended/unrecognized activity with no `actionIdentity` **and** a known MAI-001 activity whose `actionIdentity` lacks sufficient authorized Rule coverage (e.g. `CYCLING`); known identity is never, by itself, Safety clearance — never one Rule per activity, never a medical-inference engine; `DEFERRED` does not imply permanent silence.

**General Product Principle (FITME-wide, not unique to this vertical):** FITME is an actively-involved coach from the beginning of the relationship, in every coaching domain it governs — asking questions, seeking information, making bounded interventions, and adapting over time. Early relationship does not mean silence. When FITME has a legitimate, governed reason to engage in its coaching domains, lack of established Trust alone must not create a generic silence requirement. ## Fixed Product Reason: `ADAPT_TO_CURRENT_STATE` (Final Revision)

Following a dedicated read-only investigation into the repository's authoritative Product Reason vocabulary (D1-IE-01's seven-value closed enumeration, Constitution Ch.12 §12.3, `contextualMeaningPolicy.js`'s `VALID_REASON_CATEGORIES`), the Head of Product + AI Architect authorized a new canonical Product Reason for this vertical: **`ADAPT_TO_CURRENT_STATE`.**

**Canonical semantic meaning:** FITME may intervene when an established or legitimately anticipated coaching activity/action may need to be adapted to the user's relevant current state. This explains **why** bounded reasoning is justified — it does **not** assert that adaptation is actually required, does **not** determine **what** action should be proposed, does not mean the user is deviating from a goal, and does not mean the user is recovering from a behavioral setback. Bounded reasoning may still conclude, depending on context: proceed with planned training, shorten training, reduce demand/intensity, substitute another appropriate activity, light activity/walking, delay/postpone, recovery/rest, clarification, no viable proposal/Silence, or another bounded professionally-appropriate proposal already permitted by the frozen V1 action envelope (Chapter 10) — the Reason predetermines none of these outcomes.

**Why none of the seven existing Product Reasons may be reused, verified directly against repository evidence:** `SUPPORT_RECOVERY`'s own canonical definition requires *"distinguishing 'ordinary improvement' from 'recovery after a genuine prior setback' — current single-value Trajectory does not capture a two-point history"* [`FITME_Coach_Semantic_Foundation_Canonical_Decision_Package_v1.0.md §13.4`] — a **behavioral-consistency setback-recovery** concept, never physical/muscular training recovery; reusing it for TR&R would silently reinterpret, not merely extend, an already-canonically-defined token. `PREPARE_FOR_FORESEEABLE_CHALLENGE` and `PREVENT_PREDICTABLE_MISTAKE` are both tied to the same goal-`DEVIATING`/`WORSENING` Trajectory finding [`§13.1, §13.5`] — a goal-alignment-deviation concept, not a state-modulation-of-an-already-good-pattern concept. `CELEBRATE_MEANINGFUL_PROGRESS`, `PROTECT_STATED_LONG_TERM_GOALS`, and `HELP_BEFORE_DIFFICULT_DECISION` are mismatched on their own distinct terms (celebration restraint; `goal`/`goalKcal` deviation threshold; `DECISION_WINDOW`-source exclusivity with no live detector). **`REQUEST_SIGNIFICANTLY_IMPROVING_INFORMATION` retains its own existing, narrow meaning** (Habit-sourced `FOOD_LOGGING` `WEAKENING`, CSF §13.6, CLOSED) **and is not the actionable TR&R Product Reason** — it remains legitimately usable, unmodified, wherever a genuine information-request/clarification Need of its own exact existing kind arises, entirely separately from `ADAPT_TO_CURRENT_STATE`.

# 09. Decision 5 — Recovery/Rest Is Not An Activity, and Open-Ended Physical Activity Representation

## Product Decision (restated)

Recovery/rest is a legitimate coaching action. It must not be added to the activity vocabulary merely to fit the current `actionIdentity` shape. The architecture must remain capable of representing coaching actions that are not physical activities, without overbuilding a large Action Type taxonomy in V1. An absent activity identity must never automatically mean "safe," and rest/recovery representation must never provide a mechanism for disguising or bypassing Safety review of an actual activity proposal.

**Extended by a subsequent Product/Architecture decision (Open-Ended Physical Activity, this revision):** FITME must not be conceptually or behaviorally limited to MAI-001's current six activity tokens — those tokens are **not** FITME's sports universe and must not become a universal sports ontology. The bounded reasoning model may propose any professionally appropriate physical activity (e.g., Pilates, yoga, tennis, basketball, skiing, elliptical), not only the six MAI-001 currently represents — but an activity outside the six must never bypass governance or Safety merely because MAI-001 lacks a token for it, must never be blindly passed through, and must never be permanently rejected outright. No Universal Sports Ontology, no exhaustive activity catalog, no medical inference engine, no hundreds of new tokens.

**Also extended: Planned Training Identity.** Training Readiness & Recovery V1 retains "proceed with planned training," "shorten planned training," and "reduce planned training demand/intensity" as real V1 capabilities — they are **not** removed merely because the repository does not always know which activity the user planned. When the relevant activity is already known from trustworthy available context, FITME may use it. When activity identity is materially required for governance/Safety but is not known, **FITME must not guess** — it may instead use the already-approved CLARIFICATION outcome to ask the user what they planned, consistent with the Active Observer/active-involvement philosophy (Chapter 05): asking and learning when information is missing, never fabricating it, never automatically falling silent.

## Current repository constraint/gap

`actionIdentity` is, by its own module header, a vocabulary of "what the action IS" — `RUNNING`/`WALKING`/`CYCLING`/`SWIMMING`/`STRENGTH_TRAINING`/`PADEL` [`activityIdentityVocabulary.js:8, 34-41`] — a genuine physical-activity vocabulary, not an action-nature classifier. No existing field distinguishes "no activity because this is a rest proposal" from "no activity because this Candidate never carried one" (e.g., the one live G-2 info-request Candidate) from "no activity because a real activity proposal failed to populate one" — all three collapse today into the same absent-`actionIdentity` shape, which is precisely the ambiguity that would let a genuine activity proposal escape Safety review by construction, not by anyone's intent. No existing `Candidate`/`DetectedOpportunity` field carries an action-nature classification of any kind — confirmed by direct inspection of `initiativeEngine.js`'s Candidate construction [`initiativeEngine.js:330-370`] and `recommendationEngine.js`'s own construction: neither carries anything beyond `kind: 'INITIATIVE'`/`'Recommendation'`, which distinguishes *producer*, not action nature.

**MAI-001 has zero live production consumers today**, confirmed by direct search: `safetyLayer.js` — the only file besides `activityIdentityVocabulary.js` itself touching `actionIdentity` — does not `require()` the vocabulary module at all; it performs its own independent inline check, `candidate.actionIdentity.activity !== 'RUNNING'` [`safetyLayer.js:188`], never calling `isValidActionIdentity()`. MAI-001's own header already discloses this: *"No production call site exists yet in V1 (MAI-001 §15) — this module has zero live Candidate producer and zero live consumer."* This vertical will be MAI-001's **first** real production caller. MAI-001's own SPEC already frames its six tokens as an *"initial activity vocabulary... closed as stated, does not authorize Safety rules for all six, not to be expanded without further evidence/authorization"* [`MAI_001_SPEC_v1.0.md:73-75`] — repository evidence is directly consistent with, and already explicitly states, that these six tokens are the minimum identities a real deterministic consumer needed, never a claimed universe.

**A directly reusable repository precedent already exists** for splitting a closed, deterministic dimension from an open, preserved, literal value: USC-001's own `userSafetyContext` items carry a closed `restrictionClassification` dimension **alongside** an open, literal, verbatim `restrictedActivityText` field; CSR-001's own matcher matches that open text against a closed *accepted-form* list (`RUNNING_TEXT_ACCEPTED_FORMS`) rather than requiring the user's own words to already be a closed token [`safetyContextInterpreter.js`, `safetyLayer.js:134-157`]. AD-SF-04 (SFCD §06) is directly on point and explicitly forbids the naive alternative: *"Domain/Topic identifies the decision/coaching subject. Activity identity identifies the concrete action modality/activity. These are separate semantic layers. Do not modify `domainTopicVocabulary.js` to add RUNNING/WALKING/etc."* — confirming that Domain/Topic is never the right place to represent activity-level detail of any kind.

## Minimum additive architecture

**Ownership precision:** the `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` vs. `NON_ACTIVITY_COACHING_ACTION` classification, the open semantic activity reference, and the (optional) `actionIdentity` all belong to the **proposed action** — i.e., the concrete proposal the reasoning component produces and the Candidate that carries it — never to the Need/`DetectedOpportunity` as such. A `DetectedOpportunity` establishes only that a legitimate Need exists (CARF Ch.04); it does not inherently know or own what action, if any, will be proposed for it. Where the existing G-2 precedent already places a concrete `proposedAction` string on `DetectedOpportunity` itself [`initiativeEngine.js:466`], that is a narrow, existing exception specific to this pipeline's own already-closed carry-through design (`proposedAction` flows unchanged from `DetectedOpportunity` to `Candidate.action` today) — not evidence that a Need, as a concept, owns action-nature semantics. This Package does not authorize attaching `actionCategory` (or the fields below) to `DetectedOpportunity` as a general Need-level property; it belongs wherever a concrete proposed action already, honestly exists.

**(a) A new, closed, additive field on the proposed action/Candidate — conceptually `actionCategory`** (naming left to the implementing SPEC) — sibling to `actionIdentity`, never nested inside it, never modifying MAI-001's own closed shape. Minimum closed vocabulary, at the conceptual level: `{PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY, NON_ACTIVITY_COACHING_ACTION}` (exact tokens deferred to implementing SPEC).

**(b) A new, always-present, open, literal activity reference** for any `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal — conceptually an *open semantic activity reference* (e.g., `"Pilates"`), the model's own stated activity, preserved verbatim — mirroring exactly how `restrictedActivityText` is preserved verbatim on `userSafetyContext` items today. This field has no dedicated vocabulary owner and is not itself closed; it exists precisely so the model's reasoning is never artificially restricted to a Product-authored list of sports.

**(c) `actionIdentity` (MAI-001, unmodified) is populated only when a deterministic, engineering-owned normalization function maps the open reference to one of MAI-001's closed tokens** — the model never self-authoritatively assigns the deterministic MAI token; normalization is FITME-owned and deterministic, mirroring how `matchesAcceptedForm()` already does deterministic literal-form matching rather than trusting a classifier's own output [`safetyLayer.js:146-152`].

**The governing invariant, corrected from the prior version of this chapter (superseding it) — fixed here as a binding Product/Architecture decision, not an implementation detail:**

- Every `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal/Candidate **MUST** carry the open semantic activity reference (b) — always, unconditionally.
- A `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal/Candidate **MAY** additionally carry a valid `actionIdentity` (c) when deterministic normalization succeeds.
- Absence of `actionIdentity` on a `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal is **legitimate**, not a contract violation, when the open reference does not currently normalize to a known MAI-001 token — this is the intended representation for an open-ended activity (Pilates, yoga, tennis, ...) MAI-001 does not yet represent.
- Absence of `actionIdentity` **MUST NOT** be treated, by any downstream consumer, as "Safety cleared." Nor may the **presence** of a recognized `actionIdentity` be treated as Safety clearance by itself — a known MAI-001 token is not the same thing as deterministic Safety coverage for that token. Both cases activate the Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage treatment (Chapter 10) whenever deterministic Safety compatibility cannot otherwise be established for the proposal — never silent `UNMODIFIED` clearance by omission or by mere token recognition.
- `NON_ACTIVITY_COACHING_ACTION` (e.g., recovery/rest) remains structurally distinct: it carries **no** open activity reference and **no** `actionIdentity` — it must never fabricate either merely to satisfy a shape check.
- Clarification and no-viable-proposal/Silence remain structurally distinct from both of the above, exactly as already designed (Chapter 07/CARF Ch.09) — the existing G-2 info-request precedent continues to carry neither field.
- Every other existing Candidate kind (e.g., the one live G-2 info-request) is unaffected — these fields are optional/absent for any Candidate this Package's vertical does not itself produce, exactly as `actionIdentity` already is today.

**Planned-activity handling (CLARIFICATION path):** for capabilities requiring a *specific, currently-planned* activity identity ("proceed with planned training," "shorten," "reduce demand") where FITME has no trustworthy signal for which activity that is, the reasoning component's own bounded output resolves to the existing, already-precedented CLARIFICATION outcome (Chapter 07/CARF Ch.09) rather than fabricating an `actionIdentity` or an open reference. No new outcome token, no new mechanism — this reuses the same closed outcome vocabulary Decision 3 already establishes.

## What remains unchanged

MAI-001's own closed `{activity}` shape — not extended, not modified, not given a second key; its six-token list remains **FITME's closed deterministic activity-identity/normalization vocabulary** — not a guarantee of deterministic Safety coverage for every token it contains (a token being recognized is not the same thing as a Canonical Safety Rule existing for it, Chapter 10(c)), and not a claim about the universe of activities FITME can *reason about*. `isValidActionIdentity()` — unmodified, still rejects any extra key. `domainTopicVocabulary.js`/EUR-001 — untouched; AD-SF-04 is reaffirmed, not reopened. Safety Layer's own unconditional review of every Candidate regardless of `actionIdentity` presence, per CARF Chapter 11 — already true today, not a new behavior; this Package's contribution is the new *validation invariant* and the new open-reference field feeding Safety a more honest input, not a change to Safety's own unconditional-review discipline.

## What is deferred beyond V1

Any richer `actionCategory` taxonomy beyond the two-value minimum. The exact deterministic normalization algorithm/module (Chapter 15). Any expansion of MAI-001's own six-token list to a seventh/eighth token, for a specific activity **not already among the existing six** — permitted by MAI-001's own original design ("not to be expanded without further evidence/authorization") but not performed by this Package; each such expansion remains its own future, narrow, Product-authorized addition. **Precision note: `WALKING` is not such an expansion.** `WALKING` is already one of MAI-001's existing six tokens, unchanged by this Package. # 10. Decision 6 — Two-Layer Safety

## Product Decision (restated)

The model may receive relevant Safety context before reasoning, to avoid generating obviously incompatible proposals — but model awareness is never Safety authority. Every actionable proposal remains subject to FITME's deterministic Safety governance, unconditionally, before it can become a delivered decision. Deterministic Safety is extended only where a real structured/literal boundary requires it — never by relying on prompt compliance, never by treating "the model already saw the restriction" as sufficient, never by enumerating every possible coaching situation as deterministic logic.

**Extended by three subsequent Product/Architecture decisions (this revision):** **WALKING Safety** — WALKING remains a legitimate V1 proposal; the existing literal medical-restriction principle already proven for RUNNING must extend consistently to WALKING: an explicit literal WALKING restriction with valid medical provenance must be deterministically capable of conflicting with a WALKING proposal, reusing the existing CSR-001/SL-001 authority model, never inferring a restriction from pain/soreness/fatigue/symptoms/general health statements. **Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage (generalized — corrects a prior, too-narrow framing of this same principle)** — the governing question is never *"does FITME recognize this activity?"* — it is *"can FITME deterministically establish the Safety disposition of this `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal against the relevant authoritative Safety context?"* This applies to **every** `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal requiring Safety evaluation, regardless of whether `actionIdentity` is present: an open-ended/unrecognized activity with no `actionIdentity` **and** a known MAI-001 activity that has an `actionIdentity` but lacks sufficient authorized Canonical Safety Rule coverage for the relevant Safety context (e.g. `CYCLING`, `SWIMMING`, `STRENGTH_TRAINING`, `PADEL` — recognized tokens with no Rule of their own) are both governed by the same three-way principle below. Known activity identity is never, by itself, deterministic Safety coverage. FITME must not guess in either case — the intended V1 direction is a minimal, generic, activity-agnostic `DEFERRED` treatment, never one handcrafted Rule per activity, never a medical-inference engine, never an attempt to infer whether a given activity is medically safe from general medical knowledge. `DEFERRED` does not mean permanent silence (below).

## Current repository constraint/gap

The first layer (model awareness) already exists and requires no new plumbing: `pipelineContext.userSafetyContext`/`.userSafetyProvenance` (USC-001/USP-001) already flow into the Reasoning Context when domain-relevant, per CARF Chapter 07/11, unmodified. The second layer (deterministic review) is real and unconditional today [`safetyLayer.js`'s `disqualify()`/`finalReview()`, CARF Ch.11] — but its **actual coverage** is narrow: `matchCanonicalSafetyRules()` implements exactly one Canonical Safety Rule, RUNNING against an explicit literal RUNNING restriction [`safetyLayer.js:183-188`]; every other MAI-001 token (`WALKING`/`CYCLING`/`SWIMMING`/`STRENGTH_TRAINING`/`PADEL`) has zero matched-rule coverage — a Candidate carrying one of those tokens passes through Safety review (the review itself is real and unconditional) but finds nothing to match, which is functionally indistinguishable from `UNMODIFIED`/no-concern today, per SL-001's own disposition ordering [SL-001 §16].

## Minimum additive architecture

Three distinct, independently-scoped additions, none weakening SL-001/CSR-001's own closed mechanism:

**(a) The corrected Decision-5 invariant (Chapter 09) is itself part of Safety's boundary, not separate from it** — a `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal without a valid `actionIdentity`, **or with a recognized `actionIdentity` that lacks sufficient authorized Rule coverage**, is never treated as reviewed-and-cleared by that fact alone; both cases are routed to the Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage treatment below, closing both the "model already saw the restriction, so treat it as reviewed" failure mode **and** the "recognized token, so treat it as cleared" failure mode — Decision 6 explicitly forbids each.

**(b) A new Canonical Safety Rule for WALKING, CSR-001-pattern — ratified in principle.** Head of Product + AI Architect has approved reusing the **existing RUNNING dimension profile as WALKING's own intended analogue**: `riskType: ACTIVE_MEDICAL_INSTRUCTION_CONFLICT` (already a closed, activity-agnostic member of the 11-value `RISK_TYPES` enum, not RUNNING-specific — [`safetyLayer.js:67-71`]); `evidenceConfidence`/`correctability`/`urgency` selected by the same confirmed-active vs. temporally-unresolved logic RUNNING already uses [`safetyLayer.js:163-181`], whose own stated rationale (*"a RUNNING Candidate cannot be bounded-modified into a non-RUNNING one while preserving its own intent"* [`CSR_001_SPEC_v1.0.md §12`]) is activity-generic and applies to WALKING verbatim. This is a Product-level decision **in principle**; formal CSR-001-pattern ratification of the exact Rule text and its own closed accepted-form text list (a `WALKING_TEXT_ACCEPTED_FORMS` analogue to `RUNNING_TEXT_ACCEPTED_FORMS`) is confirmed as SPEC-level implementation evidence (Chapter 15), not a remaining open Product question. No new Safety framework is invented — this reuses CSR-001's own explicitly-anticipated extension point verbatim: *"V1 has exactly one rule; ordering among rules is not yet a live question (deferred to whenever a second rule exists — the existing `CANONICAL_SAFETY_RULE_ORDER` tie-break already anticipates this)"* [`CSR_001_SPEC_v1.0.md §20`].

**(c) The Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage Rule — renamed and generalized from the prior version of this chapter's own "unrecognized-activity Canonical Safety Rule" (superseding it), per Final Review.** *Terminology note: "unrecognized-activity" is retired as this Rule's own name — it incorrectly implied the Rule fires only when `actionIdentity` is absent. Historical references to "unrecognized-activity Canonical Safety Rule" elsewhere in this Package's own Document History (Chapter 17) describe that superseded framing and are retained there only as a historical record, not as live architecture.* A generic "unrecognized activity + any restriction on record → `DEFERRED`" rule is **not approved** and is withdrawn. Final Review found it over-broad: the mere existence of an unrelated, specific restriction (e.g., a literal RUNNING restriction) must not, by itself, defer an unrelated proposal (e.g., Pilates) — but, equally, the absence of shared literal wording between a restriction and a proposal must never be treated as proof the two are unrelated (e.g., a restriction against "activities involving jumping" is plainly relevant to a "jump rope" proposal even though "jumping" and "jump rope" share no exact token — naive token-matching would wrongly clear it).

**Scope, generalized (Final Review, this revision): this Rule applies whenever deterministic Safety compatibility/relevance must be evaluated for a `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` proposal, independent of whether `actionIdentity` is present.** Known activity identity is **not** the same thing as deterministic Safety coverage. **Worked example, resolved from the actual current architecture:** a Candidate with `actionIdentity.activity = CYCLING` (a recognized MAI-001 token), where the user has authoritative medical Safety-restriction context potentially relevant to cycling, and the repository has no authorized CYCLING Canonical Safety Rule capable of resolving that restriction — **CYCLING must not pass merely because it is a recognized MAI-001 token.** No Rule exists to establish outcome 1 (below) for it, and a real restriction exists such that outcome 2 cannot be asserted either — this resolves to outcome 3, `DEFERRED`, exactly as an open-ended, unrecognized activity would. The identical principle applies to `SWIMMING`, `STRENGTH_TRAINING`, `PADEL`, and any other known-or-open-ended `PHYSICAL_ACTIVITY` lacking sufficient authorized deterministic Safety coverage for the relevant context. **The Rule must distinguish three outcomes, never collapsing them:**

1. **Clear deterministic conflict** — established only where an authorized, Rule-backed literal/accepted-form match exists (the same discipline the existing RUNNING/WALKING Rules already use for their own known tokens) — resolves via existing deterministic Safety handling (SL-001's own Matrix), unchanged.
2. **Clear deterministic no-relevant-restriction case** — established only where no genuinely relevant restriction can be honestly asserted, at minimum whenever the user has **no** `userSafetyContext` restriction on record at all — ordinary Safety flow continues (`UNMODIFIED`), mirroring `matchRunningMedicalRestrictionRule()`'s own existing no-match preconditions [`safetyLayer.js:194-199`].
3. **Relevance/compatibility cannot be deterministically established** — the honest default whenever neither 1 nor 2 can be asserted (including every case where a restriction exists on record and the proposed activity does not correspond to any authorized, Rule-backed match) — resolves to SL-001's own existing, unmodified `DEFERRED` disposition, reusing its closed semantics verbatim: *"critical safety context is missing... safe classification cannot yet be established"* [SL-001 Ch.16] — never a new disposition, never a new `reasonCode`.

**The governing principle, fixed here as binding, not delegable:** lack of literal/token overlap between a restriction's own text and the proposal's own open reference is **not, by itself, proof of safety**, and does not, by itself, establish outcome 2. Literal/accepted-form matching **may** help establish outcome 1 where an authorized Rule already supports that specific match; its **absence never, by itself, establishes** outcome 2. No medical inference, no model-owned Safety judgment, no universal sports ontology, no universal medical-restriction ontology, one per-sport handcrafted Rule, and no heuristic pretending to establish medical compatibility from general knowledge are authorized to bridge this gap — where relevance is genuinely unresolved, that uncertainty is preserved honestly (outcome 3), never guessed away in either direction. **The implementing SPEC may define the minimum deterministic mechanics/representation necessary to encode this three-way distinction (e.g., a small additive deterministic status/result), but MUST NOT change the authority semantics above** — this is SPEC-delegable naming/shape only (Chapter 15), not a new taxonomy authorized by this Package. This Rule remains explicitly **not** a generalized literal-text match between the model's own reference and every restriction's own literal text as a standalone conflict-detector (a richer, plausible future enhancement carrying real synonym/false-positive risk) — that remains out of scope for V1 (Chapter 14); it is used here only as one *possible* SPEC-level input toward outcome 1, never toward outcome 2 alone.

## Safety `DEFERRED` Does Not Imply Permanent Silence — Approved Lifecycle (Option A)

A Safety `DEFERRED` disposition means the actionable proposal cannot currently be delivered as Safety-cleared for **this Decision Pass** — it does not mean FITME must disappear or remain permanently silent going forward. The exact lifecycle is now fixed, per Final Review, not delegated:

- Safety `DEFERRED` resolves, via existing, unmodified Decision Formation logic, to `kind: 'SILENCE'` for this Decision Pass [`decisionFormation.js:184-186`] — unchanged.
- Per Expression's own existing, closed contract, a `SILENCE`-kind Terminal Decision produces **no** Delivery Intent and **no** Expression output for this proposal (TASK_006_SPEC_v1.0.md §25.4) — unchanged.
- **This Decision Pass ends here.** The disqualified Candidate is never mutated, replaced, or resurrected within the same pass — Candidate immutability and D2 Unit 09's own one-Terminal-Decision-per-pass invariant are both preserved, unmodified. **No new Candidate kind, no new Terminal Decision kind, and no durable "clarification owed" persistence are authorized.** Clarification does not occur by transforming or repairing this Candidate, and no same-cycle second Terminal Decision is ever formed.
- A **later, ordinary Decision Pass** — occurring through FITME's own normal cycle, never a special retry trigger — may evaluate the same or a related Need again, from that cycle's own fresh context. Nothing suppresses this: a `DEFERRED`/`SILENCE` outcome writes no feedback event of any kind (no Delivery Intent means no dismiss/accept gesture is ever possible for it), so no explicit-request/receptiveness suppression mechanism is ever triggered by it.
- On that later, ordinary pass, the bounded AI reasoning component — already, under CARF's own existing, unmodified authority, the owner of *"whether more information is needed / whether to ask a question instead"* [CARF Ch.05] — may itself resolve to the existing CLARIFICATION outcome instead of re-proposing the same unresolved actionable recommendation. That clarification then follows the entirely ordinary, already-governed path: bounded reasoning → validated clarification proposal/Candidate → ordinary Prioritization/Winner Selection/Decision Formation/Safety review → Expression renders the already-decided clarification → the user responds → the response enters FITME's normal state/memory through the ordinary authoritative path → a further, ordinary future Decision Pass reasons again from that richer context.
- **Safety does not author the clarification. Decision Formation does not author the clarification. Expression does not invent or repair it. The model never overrides or reinterprets the prior Safety disposition. The prior Candidate remains immutable; the prior Decision Pass remains complete and untouched.**

**Not same-cycle re-proposal:** this lifecycle does not constitute same-cycle re-proposal/retry under CARF Chapter 16 item 5 — the `DEFERRED` Decision Pass ends completely; any clarification or later actionable proposal occurs in a separate, later, ordinary Decision Pass using that cycle's own fresh context. CARF Chapter 16 item 5's own general same-cycle re-proposal question remains open (Chapter 12) — this vertical simply does not require resolving it for Safety-`DEFERRED` handling.

**Binding Product requirement — clarification preference, to avoid repeated `DEFERRED` loops:** when bounded Reasoning has context showing that an actionable Training Readiness proposal cannot responsibly proceed because Safety relevance/compatibility remains unresolved, and additional user information could materially resolve that uncertainty, Reasoning **SHOULD** prefer the CLARIFICATION outcome over blindly re-proposing the same unresolved actionable recommendation. This is **not** a retry loop, **not** Safety-generated coaching, **not** deterministic professional reasoning, and **not** permission for the model to override Safety — it is a bounded Reasoning behavioral requirement, exercised fresh each ordinary Decision Pass, consistent with CARF's own already-approved authority (AI MAY PROPOSE whether more information is needed). If clarification is not possible or appropriate, the existing fail-closed Silence behavior remains valid. **The implementing SPEC must encode this requirement explicitly in the bounded reasoning contract/prompt and test it** — this is the one remaining SPEC-delegable mechanic; the lifecycle shape itself is fixed above, not delegable.

## H. CARF Chapter 16 — Which Items These Decisions Resolve

See Chapter 12 below for the full seven-item reconciliation.

## I. Open-Ended Physical Activity — Shared Semantic Reference and Ownership Boundary

Resolved by Chapter 09/10: **one minimum semantic activity reference travels through every layer without becoming a universal ontology.** Ownership is split, not merged: MAI-001 remains the sole owner of the **closed deterministic activity-identity** dimension (its shape, its six tokens, its validator — untouched; a closed identity/normalization vocabulary, not a guarantee of deterministic Safety coverage for every token it contains — Chapter 10(c)); a new, small, dependency-free normalization module owns the **deterministic mapping** from an open reference to a known token, when one exists — never a sports ontology, never activity-specific coaching logic; the **open literal reference itself has no dedicated owner module** — it is simply preserved wherever the model's own reasoning output already carries it, exactly as `restrictedActivityText` has none today. This single reference — closed token *when known* + open literal reference *always* — is the one anchor used by: the reasoning component's own output (Chapter 07/09); the Candidate (`actionIdentity`/reference pair, Chapter 09); Safety's own matching (Chapter 10, Rule-covered tokens and the generalized Unresolved Activity Safety Coverage Rule, applying identically whether or not the reference happens to normalize to a recognized token); the new preference interpreter's own extracted subject (J below); and the new explicit-opposition control's own scope resolution (J below). No subsystem invents an incompatible representation of "what activity this is."

## J. User-Stated Activity Preference / Explicit Opposition Routing

Three semantically distinct classes, per Product Decision, none collapsed into another:

**A — User-stated preference** ("I don't like running," "I love Pilates"). **FACT:** the existing free-text "add fact" Typed Memory UI already captures such statements verbatim [`js/memory.js:386`], but they do not currently reach canonical Reasoning Context — USM-001's own SPEC is explicit: *"USM-001 data does not currently reach... Pipeline Context (`assembleContext()`)"* [`AFFIRMATIVE_TRUST_V1_SPEC_v1.0.md §4.5`]. **Minimum additive architecture:** a new, narrow, sibling bounded interpreter (a sixth of the CSSC-001/EUR-001/USC-001/USP-001/Decision-2-interpreter family), reusing the same Typed-Memory-read capability, producing a Tier-5/Inference, `interpretationAuthority: 'DERIVED_INTERPRETATION'` classification of activity-relevant sentiment — Reasoning-Context-only, non-authoritative, never written back as memory, never a deterministic gate. Statement Authority ≠ Interpretation Authority is preserved by construction: "the user said this" stays Path-A authoritative; "therefore this activity is forbidden" is never derived by this interpreter — it produces a bounded sentiment signal for the model to weigh, nothing more. **This does not require, and does not implement, Preference V1** (Chapter 13, K below) — it requires no ranking/selection algorithm, only one more bounded Reasoning-Context input, structurally identical in kind to `userSafetyContext`.

**B — Explicit activity-specific opposition** ("Don't suggest cycling," "Don't recommend running to me."). **FACT, verified directly:** EUR-001's own classifier prompt already names *"running, swimming, morning workouts"* as its own worked example of a scope that resolves `UNRESOLVED` — *"the named scope is absent from, ambiguous within, or broader than this closed list... answer 'scopeStatus': 'UNRESOLVED'"* [`explicitRequestInterpreter.js:150-157`] — confirming EUR-001 already, correctly, detects the suppression *intent* but cannot resolve activity-level *scope*, by design. AD-SF-04 forbids the naive fix (adding activity-level Topics to `domainTopicVocabulary.js`). **Minimum additive architecture, now authorized:** a new, sibling, activity-specific explicit-opposition control — reusing EUR-001's own proven skeleton (id-keyed batching, fail-closed, no retry) but resolving its own "scope" dimension against the shared semantic activity reference (I above) instead of Domain/Topic. **Deterministic enforcement is required, not merely prompt context** — model awareness alone repeats exactly the failure mode Decision 6 already rejected for Safety, generalized here: a new, small, deterministic gate, structurally parallel to `explicitlyRequestedAgainst()` [`initiativeEngine.js:326`], checked before/at Candidate construction, suppressing a proposal whose activity reference matches a real, sourced, activity-specific opposition record.

**C — Safety restriction** ("My doctor told me not to run.") — unchanged, remains exclusively under USC-001/USP-001/CSR-001/Safety authority (Chapters 09/10). Never collapsed with A or B.

**Deterministic enforcement summary:** Preference (A) — none, advisory Reasoning-Context input only. Explicit opposition (B) — yes, a new deterministic gate. Safety restriction (C) — yes, the existing/extended CSR-001/SL-001 model. This split requires no unpausing of Preference V1 (K below).

## K. Preference V1 Remains PAUSED

**Confirmed, not reopened.** Preference V1 (the paused Work Item) is specifically a *ranking/selection* capability among multiple already-valid, already-Safety-reviewed alternatives sharing one same-Need identity — CARF's own framing: *"Preference would select among already professionally-plausible, already-Safety-reviewed alternatives... never among Candidates from unrelated Needs"* [CARF Ch.13]. What J-A/J-B above require is categorically narrower: the model *seeing* one more bounded, literal, Reasoning-Context input (no FITME-side ranking/scoring algorithm), and a binary deterministic suppress/allow gate for explicit opposition (not a selection algorithm among several already-valid alternatives). Neither requires multiple simultaneous alternatives, a learned-scoring model, or any of Preference V1's own actual design surface. Implicit learned preference (e.g., inferring dislike from repeated dismissals) remains explicitly outside this Package's own scope, per Product's own instruction, unless separately authorized later.
